By train · recommended

Leave Zürich HB at the front, Bahnhofplatz, and cross the Bahnhofbrücke to Central. Walk along Neumühlequai, take the stairs up to Walchestrasse and follow it to Wasserwerkstrasse 6. About 12 minutes on foot.

Walking route →

Map: Google

By bus

Bus 46 from Bahnhofquai/HB, one stop to Stampfenbachplatz, then about 4 minutes on foot. Barely faster than walking.

By car

No parking can be reserved. The hotel's public car park and the car parks nearby will most likely be full early. Please come by train.

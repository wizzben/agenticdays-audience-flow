What am I looking at?

The hotel venue in 3D: main building (left, back) and north building (front), with the skywalk and escalator between them. Dots are people. The coloured “liquid” in each room shows how full it is.

  • Open – seats available
  • Filling – few seats left
  • Full – no seats left (50-seat rooms: demand beyond capacity is likely)
  • Running – session started

Measured vs. estimated vs. projected

  • Measured: only each room’s level (open / filling / full / running), polled every 30 s by a recorder.
  • Estimated: headcounts and movement. Levels are mapped to bands of the room capacity (theatre seating per the hotel's event brochure: Millennium 650, Studio 4 120, Studio 3 100, Studio 1 and Studio 6 60 each), smoothed by timing, and the 500 attendees are moved between rooms and hubs so that nothing is lost or duplicated.
  • Projected: where there is no recording (future or gaps), shown hatched / violet-tinted, based on schedule, capacity and topic popularity so far. Not measured.

Topic clouds

20 topics extracted from the session titles and descriptions: recurring words are grouped under a general name, and a session only feeds a topic when its own text contains those words. Size = estimated audience-minutes so far (or people in rooms now). Hover or tap one to light up the rooms and sessions that feed it.

Controls

Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, right-drag to pan, click a room to focus it. Space play/pause · ←/→ ±1 min (Shift ±10) · [/] previous/next session change · L live · R reset.
